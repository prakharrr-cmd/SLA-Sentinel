# SLA-Sentinel

Business Operations Intelligence & Decision Engine

SLA Sentinel is a business operations intelligence platform designed to help support and operations teams monitor Service Level Agreement (SLA) performance, identify at-risk tickets, understand operational bottlenecks, and make evidence-based decisions.

It transforms support-ticket data into actionable operational insights through a structured workflow combining dataset ingestion, SLA risk analysis, explainable recommendations, manager decisions, action tracking, and outcome evaluation.
